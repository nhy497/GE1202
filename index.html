Key Formulas to Remember

  • Ch2 Liquidity Ratio = Liquid Assets ÷ Monthly Expenses
  • Ch3 Compound Interest: More frequent = Higher yield
  • Ch4 APR = (Average Annual Finance Charges ÷ Average Credit Used) × 100%
  • Ch5 Max LTV = 60% (HK$7M below), Down Payment = 40%
  • Ch6 Life Insurance = 5-10× Annual Income (Rule of Thumb)
  • Ch7 Margin % = Equity ÷ Stock Value

Critical Numbers

  • 3-6 months: Emergency fund (savings rule of thumb)
  • 30-90 days: Single-payment loan typical duration
  • HK$50 or 2-3%: Credit card minimum payment
  • 20-25 days: Credit card grace period
  • 40%: Margin maintenance requirement
  • 1%: Property agent commission

Study Tips

Priority Focus (Ch6-7)

These chapters have detailed concepts on Insurance techniques and Investment strategies. Master the 5 risk management methods and margin trading calculations.

Foundation (Ch2-5)

Understand the 4 financial ratios, 3-6 month emergency fund rule, loan types (30-90 days), and HKMA mortgage guidelines (60% LTV).

Calculation Practice

Practice APR calculations, margin trading examples (Ch7 p.22-23), and liquidity ratio formulas. These often appear in exams.
