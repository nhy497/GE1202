Chapter 7: Investment

Stocks, Bonds, Margin Trading (50%/40%)

Investment vs Speculation

Investment

  • Long-term perspective
  • Value stability and predictable return levels
  • Process of purchasing securities

Speculation

  • Short-term buying/selling
  • Future value and returns highly uncertain
  • High risk, high return

Investment Requirements

Investment Capital

Accumulated funds beyond emergency savings

Knowledge

Understand security types, markets, trading strategies

Self-Knowledge

Risk tolerance, investment objectives

Experience

Paper trades (simulated trading)

Golden Rule: NEVER invest in something you don't understand!

Two Methods to Accumulate Investment Capital

1

Lump Sum Investment

Pre-invest a sum of money and let it grow over time

  • • Requires larger initial capital
  • • Compound growth starts immediately
  • • Suitable for those with existing savings
2

Systematic Savings Plan

Deposit a fixed amount regularly each year

  • • Lower barrier to entry, easy to start
  • • Dollar-cost averaging effect
  • • Builds saving habits

Higher expected return rate = less initial capital needed

Types of Securities Comparison

Common Stock

Equity form - ownership interest

  • Return Dividends + Price appreciation
  • Features High return potential, high risk
  • Maturity No maturity date
  • Rights Voting rights to elect management

Bonds

Liability - issuer's IOU

  • Return Fixed interest income
  • Features Stable returns, lower risk
  • Maturity Fixed maturity date
  • Relation Price moves opposite to interest rates

Preferred & Convertibles

Hybrid securities - combine debt and equity

  • Preferred Fixed dividends, priority over common stock
  • Convertible Can be converted to common stock
  • Advantage Income + Price appreciation potential

Risk vs Return Comparison

Bonds

Low risk, low return

Preferred Stock

Medium risk/return

Common Stock

High risk, high return

Speculation

Very high risk/return
