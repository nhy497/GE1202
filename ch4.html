Chapter 4

Consumer Credits & Loans

APR, Closed-end vs Open-end Credit, 30-90 Day Loans

Key Concepts

  • Closed-end One-time loan with fixed payments over specified period
  • Open-end Continuous borrowing with periodic partial payments (e.g., credit cards)
  • Credit Card Min payment: HK$50 or 2-3% of balance (whichever is HIGHER)

Key Points

  • Three closed-end credits (Single-payment, Instalment sales, Instalment cash)
  • Credit card grace period: 20-25 days
  • APR example: 18% (credit cards)
  • Home equity line: 75% loan-to-value ratio

Three Types of Closed-end Credit

1. Single Lump-sum Credit

  • • Must repay IN FULL on specified date
  • • Typical term: 30-90 days
  • • Best for: Short-term cash needs

2. Instalment Sales Credit

  • • Pay down payment first, balance plus interest in equal instalments
  • • Purpose: Buying goods (e.g., appliances, cars)

3. Instalment Cash Credit

  • • No down payment, direct cash loan
  • • Repay balance plus interest in equal instalments

Credit Card Terms (Critical Numbers)

Minimum Payment

HK$50

OR 2-3% of balance (whichever is HIGHER)

Grace Period

20-25 days

Pay in full during period = NO interest

WARNING: Credit card rates are among the HIGHEST debt rates! Example: 18% APR

Home Equity Line of Credit - Example

Scenario: Couple bought home 10 years ago for HK$2,850,000, now worth HK$3,650,000

Remaining mortgage: HK$2,200,000

Home equity: HK$3,650,000 - HK$2,200,000 = HK$1,450,000

Available credit calculation:

75% Loan-to-Value × HK$3,650,000 = HK$2,737,500

Minus remaining mortgage: HK$2,737,500 - HK$2,200,000

= Available: HK$537,500

Quick Summary

This chapter covers consumer credit and loan types. Key points: Three closed-end credits (single-payment loans typically 30-90 days, instalment sales and cash credits), credit card terms (minimum payment HK$50 or 2-3%, 20-25 day grace period), home equity credit line (75% loan-to-value ratio calculation). Example: 18% credit card APR interest calculation.
