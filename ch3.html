Chapter 3

Saving

3-6 Month Emergency Fund Rule & Types of Savings Accounts

What is Saving?

Monthly Income > Monthly Expenditure = Saving

  • Sacrificing current consumption in favor of future benefits
  • Provides financial independence and security later in life
  • Keeps away the need to resort to expensive loans or emergency lenders

Key Points

  • 3-6 months income as emergency fund
  • Compound interest (8%-21% annual return examples)
  • Liquidity Ratio = Liquid Assets / Monthly Expenses
  • Saving vs Investment time horizon difference

THE GOLDEN RULE: Emergency Fund

Readily available savings should equal

3 - 6 months

of your income as safety buffer

Calculation:
Liquidity Ratio = Liquid Assets ÷ Monthly Expenses
Goal:
High liquidity ratio = ability to pay unexpected expenses

Quick Summary

Golden Rule: Maintain readily available savings equal to 3-6 months of income. This chapter covers the importance of saving, including liquidity ratio calculation (liquid assets ÷ monthly expenses), compound interest effects (Dow Jones 1987-2012 averaged 8% annual return, Buffett 1967-2007 averaged 21.1%), and how to establish effective saving habits.
