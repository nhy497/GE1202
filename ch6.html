Chapter 6: Insurance & Health

5 Risk Management Techniques, Life Insurance Methods

Basic Insurance Concepts

Purpose of Insurance

Protect you and dependents from financial loss when accident, illness, or death occurs

What is Risk?

Chance of economic loss - Life, Health, Home, Car, Business

Risk Management

Devise strategies for anticipating and dealing with risk exposures

5 Risk Management Techniques (MUST KNOW!)

1

Risk Avoidance

Avoid acts that create risk

2

Loss Prevention

Reduce probability of loss

3

Loss Control

Lessen severity of loss

4

Risk Assumption

Accept and bear the risk

5

Insurance

Transfer risk via insurance

Health Insurance Decision Process

Hong Kong Healthcare System Comparison

Public Healthcare System

  • Low cost
  • Resource shortage
  • Long waiting times

Private Healthcare System

  • Efficient service
  • No waiting
  • High cost

Health Insurance Decision Steps

1

Evaluate health care cost risk

Two types of losses: medical expenses + income loss

2

Determine available coverage

Public healthcare + employer basic coverage

3

Choose health insurance plan

Compare plans offered by different companies

Hospital Insurance Components

Part A Basic Hospital Insurance

  • Covers room and board
  • Other inpatient and outpatient care
  • Has deductibles (varies by length of stay)

Part B Supplementary Medical Insurance

  • Optional coverage (requires premium)
  • Covers doctors, surgeons fees
  • Lab tests, X-rays, home health care
