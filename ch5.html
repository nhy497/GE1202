Chapter 5

Purchasing Homes

HKMA Guidelines, 60% LTV, Mortgage Types

Key Concepts

  • Mortgage Ratio HKMA: Max 60% LTV for properties below HK$7M (40% down payment)
  • Agent Commission 1% of property price
  • P-rate Formula Mortgage rate = P(Prime rate) - spread (e.g., 5.25% - 3.1% = 2.15%)

Key Points

  • Loan-to-Value (LTV): 60% (properties below HK$7M)
  • Two mortgage types: Fixed-rate vs Floating-rate (P-based/HIBOR-based)
  • Amortization periods: 20, 25, 30 years
  • HK property history: 1997 peak → 2003 bottom → post-2009 rebound

Two Mortgage Types Comparison

Fixed-Rate Mortgage

  • • Interest rate FIXED for entire loan term
  • • Monthly payment stays constant
  • • Terms: 20, 25, 30 years
  • • Best for: Risk-averse borrowers

Adjustable-Rate Mortgage (ARM)

  • • Interest rate VARIES during loan life
  • • Common in HK: P-based, HIBOR-based
  • • P-based formula: Prime rate - spread
  • • Example: 5.25% - 3.1% = 2.15%

Hong Kong Property Market History

1997

Market Peak → Many homeowners had negative equity (mortgage > property value)

2003

SARS Bottom → Prices hit lowest then quickly rebounded

2009+

Long-term low interest rates fueled rapid rise

Quick Summary

Key Data: HKMA mandates max 60% LTV for properties below HK$7M (40% down payment), property agent commission 1%, P-based rate calculation (Prime rate - spread). HK property market history: 1997 peak → negative equity wave → 2003 SARS bottom → post-2009 low-rate environment fueled rapid rise. Consider opportunity costs, stamp duty, fire insurance, maintenance before purchasing.
